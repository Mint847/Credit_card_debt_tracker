    Debt-to-Income (DTI) Load

    Standard banking health metric

    This percentage represents your mandatory monthly credit card payments divided by your net monthly income.

    It assumes you are paying the standard CBSL 4% minimum across all active card balances. Banks use this exact ratio to determine if you can safely afford new loans or higher credit limits.

    Merchant Surcharge Rules

    Visa, Mastercard & CBSL Guidelines

    Under standard Visa and Mastercard network rules, merchants are generally prohibited from passing the Merchant Discount Rate (swipe fee) directly to the consumer as a line-item credit card surcharge. Knowing this true cost helps you negotiate better cash prices or identify unfair point-of-sale penalties.

    Where does your payment go?

    This visualizer breaks down exactly how your very first minimum payment is split. Because interest compounds heavily when making minimum payments, you'll often find a massive chunk of your payment instantly vanishing into interest fees, while only a small fraction is applied to the actual principal balance you owe.

    Effective APR Explained

    The Mathematical Reality

    Even though the installment plan is advertised as 0% interest, paying an upfront merchant fee means you are paying a premium to borrow that money over time. Mathematically, this acts as a hidden annualized interest rate. We calculate this true cost so you can accurately compare it against standard loans or other credit cards.
